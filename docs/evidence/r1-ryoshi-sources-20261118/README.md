# “ryo”, “nobody” and the R1 audio

Hunters asked whether the software label `RYO OS 0.43` could refer to Ryoshi. Two public sources establish that people called Ryoshi “ryo” before the first CAW riddle. One of them also contains the word in the audio’s artist field. Who chose those labels, and what `0.43` means, remain open.

## What the dated sources say

**May 29, 2021.** In *I AM RYOSHI*, Ryoshi recalled receiving messages like “ryo, you must be a billionaire now, haha”. That is how other people addressed the writer. In the same essay, Ryoshi wrote “I am a nobody”. Wayback captured the essay at 15:51:21 UTC, about 72 seconds after its recorded publication time. Both passages appear in that capture. [Original essay, archived on publication day](https://web.archive.org/web/20210529155121id_/https://allhailtheshiba.medium.com/i-am-ryoshi-5fe57841f8f1)

**April 19, 2022.** CertiK published an interview in which the host asked whether the SHIB team knew who Ryoshi was. Kaal Dhairya answered with a joke about a future “search for Ryo”. A second speaker used the same nickname in a conversation that explicitly named Ryoshi. [CertiK’s original transcript](https://certik.medium.com/security-leaderboard-showcase-ama-with-shiba-inu-recap-c03cd629062f)

**May 9, 2022.** The archived R1 tablet points to the preserved audio by its content address. Its artist field reads `nobody`. Its RIFF software field reads `RYO OS 0.43 (libsndfile-1.0.31)`, and its ID3 software field reads `RYO OS 0.43`. This dates the presence of the labels in the riddle audio. Anyone preparing the file could have read the public essay and used the same wording. [Archived tablet](https://web.archive.org/web/20220509164829id_/https://i.ibb.co/9TQPCxp/Proposalforadecentralizedcoupfortehpplbytehppl.png) · [How the image identifies the audio](../r1-independent-review-20261118/README.md)

## Why “Ryoshi Operating System” is still a theory

The nickname makes a deliberate reference plausible. The word “nobody” is commonplace. Neither source mentions an operating system or explains the version number. In a separate replay, supplying the software name `CONTROL` to the same version of libsndfile produced `CONTROL (libsndfile-1.0.31)`. The software field can carry text chosen during export. It cannot authenticate the editor, the operating system or the person who made the recording. [Software-label replay and control](../r1-upper-strip-20261118/README.md#what-ryo-os-043-tells-us)

## What was searched

Across four archived Medium essay bodies, the quoted message above was the only standalone use of “ryo”, ignoring case. We excluded those three letters inside “Ryoshi”. The other three essays can be checked in their archived originals. We also checked 37 archived post IDs from the original @RyoshiResearch account. Thirty-five contained attributable text and two contained only shortened links. None of the four essays or the 35 readable posts contained `RYO OS`, `0.43` or “operating system”. The search covers the archived text.
